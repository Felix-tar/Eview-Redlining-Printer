# Privacy Policy – eVIEW Redlining Print Assistant

Last updated: September 8, 2026

## Overview

The eVIEW Redlining Print Assistant is a browser extension designed to assist users of the eVIEW web application with identifying and printing document pages that contain redlining annotations.

The extension operates locally within the user's browser.

## Data Collection

The eVIEW Redlining Print Assistant does not collect personal information from its users.

The extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share:

- Names or contact information
- eVIEW usernames or passwords
- Authentication credentials
- Browsing history
- Financial information
- Location information
- Analytics or tracking information

## eVIEW Content

The extension may temporarily access the content of the currently opened eVIEW page when required to provide its functionality, such as:

- detecting redlining annotations
- identifying the related document pages
- adjusting the page view
- preparing pages for printing

This processing takes place locally in the user's browser.

Document contents, redlining information, screenshots, and captured document pages are not transmitted to the publisher or to external servers operated by the publisher.

## Authentication

The extension does not provide authentication to eVIEW and does not collect or process eVIEW login credentials.

Users must independently have legitimate access to an existing eVIEW environment.

The extension does not bypass authentication, access controls, or permissions.

## Local Storage

The extension may use browser-local storage for settings, preferences, temporary page information, or other data required for its normal operation.

This information remains within the user's browser and is not transmitted to the publisher.

## Third Parties

The extension does not sell or share user information with third parties.

The extension does not use:

- advertising networks
- tracking services
- external analytics services
- developer-operated cloud processing for eVIEW document content

eVIEW itself is a separate third-party service and is subject to the privacy policies and terms of its respective provider or operator.

## User Control

Users can stop the extension from accessing eVIEW at any time by disabling or uninstalling the extension through their browser.

Locally stored extension data can be removed by uninstalling the extension or clearing the extension's browser storage.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data-handling practices change.

The latest version of this policy will be published at this location.

## Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the eVIEW Redlining Print Assistant, please contact the publisher through the support information provided on the Microsoft Edge Add-ons listing.
